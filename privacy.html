Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This website belongs to Divu, a web developer in India. This page explains in simple words what information the website uses and why.

1. What I collect

This website does not collect your personal information by itself. There are no accounts, no cookies and no analytics or advertising trackers.

2. The contact form

The form does not send your message to a server. When you press Send, your email app (or WhatsApp, if it is switched on) opens with your message ready. You decide whether to send it. If you do, I receive your name, email address and message, and I use them only to reply to you and to talk about your project.

3. The price estimator

Your choices stay in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you press Send on the contact form.

4. Data saved on your device

The mini game on the FAQ section saves your best score in your browser’s local storage. It stays on your device, and you can delete it any time from your browser settings.

5. Other services

  • Google Fonts: the fonts load from Google. Google may receive your IP address and browser details to deliver them. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Hosting: the company that hosts this website may keep normal server logs, such as IP address and time of visit.
  • Email and WhatsApp: if you contact me through them, their own privacy policies apply.
  • Links to other websites: my project links lead to other websites that I do not control.

6. How long I keep messages

I keep emails and messages only for as long as I need them to reply to you and to work on a project with you.

7. Your choices

You can ask me what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Write to techhellrm017@gmail.com.

8. Children

This website is not meant for children.

9. Changes

I may update this page. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
